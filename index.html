Watched

A Short Film with a Movie Poster made using Da Vinci Resolve, Blender, and The Adobe Suite.

Created by Katie Sugrue (Student Number: 20097677), this project demonstrates my ability to use the Production Pipeline, showcase my Video Production skills, and challenge myself by learning new technologies independently. I will also be implementing 3D and 2D animation into this short film to showcase my skills within those sectors.

This Short Film highlights the dangers of social media and shows how dangerous it can be if you are not aware of internet safety.
